## 2026–2028 Strategic Roadmap & Requests for Proposals

Before submitting a Development Fund proposal, please review the Foundation's current technical roadmap and Requests for Proposals:

➡️ [2026–2028 Splice and Canton Strategic Roadmap and 2026–2027 Requests for Proposals](../2026-2028-strategic-roadmap.md)

The roadmap identifies technical areas where the Foundation is actively seeking community contributions and expects to prioritize Development Fund resources.

This directory contains Requests for Proposals aligned with the Canton Foundation Development Fund's 2026–2028 Strategic Roadmap. Each subdirectory corresponds to a major roadmap category and is the submission location for proposals responding to RFPs in that area.

Proposals that do not respond to a published RFP should continue to be submitted as individual initiatives under `/proposals/`.

### Proposal Paths

Development Fund proposals fall into two categories:

**RFP-aligned proposals**

These respond directly to a published Foundation Request for Proposals. Applicants should identify the RFP they are addressing and explain the specific problem, user need, and evidence supporting the proposed work.

**Individual initiatives**

The Development Fund remains open to community-generated proposals outside the published roadmap. Because these proposals compete with work already identified as a Foundation priority, applicants should demonstrate strong ecosystem demand, community support, and a clear Development Fund Champion.

### Before submitting

Please:

1. Review the current roadmap and RFPs.
2. Identify whether your proposal responds to an existing RFP.
3. Discuss the need with the relevant SIG, maintainers, developers, or affected ecosystem participants.
4. Gather evidence that the problem is real and sufficiently important to solve.
5. Identify a Development Fund Champion.
6. Submit your proposal using the current proposal template.

A published RFP does not guarantee funding. All proposals remain subject to technical review, milestone review, available budget, and Technology & Operations governance.
